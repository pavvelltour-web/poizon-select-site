# Unified missing-angle generation notes

These references fill missing or source-cropped views in the active five-frame
catalog sets. Each image was generated from the complete existing product
gallery for the same SKU, then visually compared with those references before
normalization.

The generation contract requires:

- exact product identity, colourway, material cues, logos and proportions;
- one requested angle only: opposite side, front or rear;
- the complete product inside the frame with generous safe margins;
- a neutral RGB `242,243,243` studio background;
- no mirroring, stretch, unrelated objects or promotional text.

These are project-generated visual references. They are not represented as
official Poizon or manufacturer photography.
